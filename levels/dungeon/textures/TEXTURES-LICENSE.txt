	How they were made, so the numbers are in one place:

	  GAMMA=1.4
	  magick "<pack>_Color.jpg"    -resize 512x512 -gamma $GAMMA -quality 85  <name>_color.jpg
	  magick "<pack>_NormalGL.jpg" -resize 512x512             -quality 92  <name>_normal.jpg

	metal_color.jpg is the exception, and gets a contrast stretch as well.
	Metal055A is nearly flat -- standard deviation 6 of 255, against brick's
	16 -- so at prop scale it read as white plastic rather than as steel:

	  magick "Metal055A_1K-JPG_Color.jpg" -resize 512x512 -gamma $GAMMA \
	         -sigmoidal-contrast 7,78% -quality 85 metal_color.jpg
	  magick metal_color.jpg -color-matrix "0.534 0 0  0 0.583 0  0 0 0.597" \
	         -quality 85 metal_dark_color.jpg
	  magick <atlas dumped from the .blend> -gamma $GAMMA -strip kit_atlas.png

	metal_rough.jpg is the one map here that is not colour and not a normal:
	Metal055A's Roughness, at 256 rather than 512. glTF packs metallic and
	roughness into one image that Blender writes as a PNG whatever it is fed,
	so the source resolution is the only lever on its size -- 512 came out
	200 KB in the glb and 256 comes out 52 KB, for something with no high
	frequencies in it. The pack's Metalness map is deliberately not shipped:
	mean 254 of 255, standard deviation 2, which a constant factor says free.

	  magick "Metal055A_1K-JPG_Roughness.jpg" -resize 256x256 -quality 88 metal_rough.jpg

	GAMMA is the one brightness knob for the whole map, applied once to the
	source rather than at render time -- see "Brightness belongs in the
	textures" in levels/AUTHORING.md. Normals are never gamma-touched: a normal
	map is a direction, not a colour, and is tagged Non-Color in Blender.

	NormalGL rather than NormalDX: glTF and three.js both read normal maps in
	the OpenGL convention, and the DX variant has its green channel inverted,
	which lights every surface from the wrong side.

	kit_atlas.png stays a PNG. Its 32 swatches are flat cells with hard edges,
	the paint eyedropper picks texels straight out of them, and JPEG would bleed
	one swatch into the next exactly where a picker lands.

	The 1K originals are not committed -- re-download from ambientCG by the id
	above if a larger source is ever needed. The other maps in each pack are
	deliberately unused: see "Which maps to ship" in levels/AUTHORING.md.
