	The lobby's models are KayKit's Dungeon Asset Pack, the same kit the
	dungeon is built from — rubble, wall, wall_doorway and floor_tile_large.
	The licence and credits are one folder over, in
	levels/dungeon/KIT-LICENSE.txt (Creative Commons Zero).

	**This level ships no textures.** Its four materials are flat colours, taken
	from the mean of the photographic maps the room used to carry and then
	desaturated a little, because a flat colour reads more saturated than a
	photograph averaging to the same value:

	  brick  #666660   the wall ring and its four doorways
	  rock   #303133   the rubble cavern, and the walls' darker trim
	  tiles  #867C70   the floor
	  wood   #4A3928   the doors' leaves

	That is deliberate and not a shortcut. The lobby is the one map whose frames
	are shared with `MapWarmer` uploading the *next* map's textures, so it is the
	worst place in the game to spend texture memory — and the detail was fighting
	the room rather than helping it.
